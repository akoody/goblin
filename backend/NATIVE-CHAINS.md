# Bitcoin and Ethereum processing

Migration 034 adds policies, durable signed jobs, source-specific fee accounting and an Ethereum finalized-block cursor. Existing addresses and encrypted customer keys are preserved. Treasury vault keys use the existing authenticated encryption with AAD `0:TREASURY:BTC` and `0:TREASURY:ETH`; retain database and WALLET_ENCRYPTION_KEY backups together.

Configuration:

- `NATIVE_CHAINS_ENABLED=true` enables the worker. SQL policies independently govern deposit-address display, new withdrawals and manual/threshold collection. Activation keeps collection manual.
- `ETHEREUM_RPC_URL=https://ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com` (Ethereum chain ID 1 only).
- `BITCOIN_API_URL=https://blockstream.info/api` (Bitcoin genesis pinned).
- `NATIVE_START_AT=YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ` starts discovery for new funds. Historic funds do not silently incur new AML checks or get credited twice. Historic unexplained balances require explicit reconciliation/backfill.

These are public third-party services without an uptime guarantee. Discovery and confirmation fail closed. Ethereum contract-funded transfers are additionally discovered through the public Ethereum Blockscout indexer. Canonical RPC receipt/block checks and exact funding reconciliation gate credit; the indexer alone cannot credit a balance. Indexed traces of one incoming transaction are grouped into one deposit per recipient. Bitcoin inputs to one recipient in one transaction likewise form one deposit, not one fee per UTXO. Discovery rotates address batches; large histories or excessive UTXOs remain quarantined for investigation. Bitcoin requires six confirmations (100 for coinbase), Ethereum finalized receipts. Blockchain reorgs and unexplained balances stop processing.

Safio capabilities for the installed account include Ethereum but not Bitcoin. Never submit BTC as TRON or fabricate a screening result. By default BTC is held for manual review with a genuine external AML report; multiple-sender ETH deposits also remain manual. For automatic BTC AML another supported provider/account is required.

Migration 035 adds an explicit, audited BTC-only `aml_disabled` option. When enabled by the full administrator, NEW BTC discoveries snapshot `deposit_reviews.aml_exempt=1` and enter `PENDING_CHAIN`. They may credit after six confirmations, exact native-funding reconciliation, user deposit permission, current BTC policy permission, fresh pricing and the ordinary minimum/fee checks. The record is `NO_AML` / `NOT_SCREENED`, never LOW risk, with a plain-language audit note. Provider calls are not made. Historical `PENDING_AML`, blocked reviews, other currencies and discoveries made before opt-out are never auto-converted. Re-enabling AML stops this path even for waiting exempt deposits. Transient chain/price failures retry after five minutes without crediting. Customer deposit UI and transaction history disclose that AML is disabled. This setting is not an assessment of source-of-funds risk.

Deposit service fee uses the existing editable deposit_policy (2 USDT equivalent by default), fixed into the review at discovery, converted at credit using a fresh quote. Default withdrawal service fee is 1 USDT equivalent, minimum recipient amount 10 USDT equivalent. Both withdrawal limits are editable per asset. Quotes last 60 seconds and separately disclose recipient amount, service fee, network expense and total liability. Confirming a quote locks the complete reserve and the source wallet. Service fee is charged only on success; actual spent network fees are not refundable on a failed mined transaction. No floating point is used for amount accounting, UTXOs, signatures or settlement.

Signed mainnet transactions are persisted before broadcast. Retries use the same serialized bytes. No automatic replacement/RBF, arbitrary force-unlock, nonce replacement or deadline-based refund is allowed. After 24 hours without confirmation, administrator review is required; the source remains guarded. Recheck retries reconciliation, not a newly signed transfer. Final settlement is transactional and idempotent. A finalized failed collection gets a one-hour source cooldown.

Consolidation uses only credited, provably attributed deposit/withdrawal fees. On-chain balance must equal the full customer liability plus uncollected fees, exactly; AML-held funds, unsupported internal-transfer history, pending operations and mismatches block collection. Existing trading/internal book balances without address provenance cannot be silently swept or externally withdrawn. Bitcoin makes a bounded multi-wallet PSBT batch (up to 10 source wallets), returns each remaining customer liability to its original P2WPKH address and sends net profit to the separate Bitcoin vault. Dust is rejected, never donated. Ethereum signs one EOA native transfer per source; contract destinations and delegated/nonstandard source accounts are rejected. Collection network expense comes from earned fees, never customer balances. A cost above policy or 30% of gathered fees is rejected. Moving custody is not a second profit event.

Threshold checks use uncollected treasury accounting as a trigger, then re-prove source readiness; an unsweepable book balance never authorizes moving other funds. Defaults: BTC threshold 0.001, source minimum 0.0001, fee ceiling 0.00005 BTC; ETH threshold 0.05, source minimum 0.0025, fee ceiling 0.001 ETH. Actual network fees may be much lower. Signed jobs retain their original fee ceiling even if settings later change. Keep thresholds economical and monitor confirmations.

Verification: `npm test`; `npm run build` from repo root. `scripts/check-native-sql.js` requires a dedicated, empty `qa_native_*` schema populated with schema only, including 034. It replaces every blockchain request, verifies both withdrawals and collections against actual MySQL, and never broadcasts real funds. Unit/mocked browser/SQL checks are not evidence of a funded mainnet transfer.

Admin endpoints under `/admin/native-chains` are full-administrator only. Helpers are denied. User quote/execute/history routes are authenticated under `/api/transactions/native`. Private keys and serialized signed jobs are not returned. Deposit/withdrawal permissions are rechecked under source locks. Quote creation is rate-limited. A new quote is required after balance, UTXO, nonce or policy changes.
